WHAT EXACTLY ARE THESE PRIMARY SOURCES?

The Adabida testimonies are authentic personal accounts of lived experiences, family memories, and oral history. As primary sources, they offer:

  • Personal perspectives on historical events
  • Emotional and human context often missing from history books
  • Details of daily life and individual experiences
  • Voices traditionally excluded from official history

WHY ARE THEY NOT VERIFIED?

Adabida is an archive of oral memory, not a certified historical archive. Its goal is to preserve memories as people experience and narrate them, respecting their emotional and narrative authenticity.

This means that:

  • Human memory is naturally subjective
  • Memories may change or transform over time
  • There may be unintentional errors in dates, names, or sequences of events
  • Adabida does not have the resources to verify every statement against historical documentation

HOW SHOULD THESE MATERIALS BE USED?

If you are a researcher, historian, journalist, or student:

  • ✓ Treat these testimonies as primary sources that require cross-checking
  • ✓ Apply critical methodology and academic rigor
  • ✓ Do not assume claims are accurate without independent verification
  • ✓ Cite the source properly, indicating that it is an unverified personal testimony
  • ✓ Remember that these are individual perceptions, not established historical facts
