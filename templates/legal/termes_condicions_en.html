5. NATURE OF THE SERVICE: ORAL MEMORY ARCHIVE

5.1. ACADEMIC NATURE

Adabida is conceived as a project with an academic vocation and historiographical rigor, aimed at creating a documentary oral memory archive accessible to researchers and to anyone wishing to preserve their legacy or that of their family.

5.2. UNVERIFIED TESTIMONIES

⚠️ IMPORTANT WARNING: The testimonies and content stored on Adabida are authentic personal memories, but they are NOT verified by professional historians.

Adabida preserves memory as people remember and share it. This means that:

  • Testimonies reflect personal perceptions and memories
  • There may be errors in dates, names or events
  • Human memory is subjective and may change over time
  • Adabida does not verify facts or cross-check sources

5.3. PRIMARY SOURCES, NOT ABSOLUTE TRUTHS

Adabida content must be treated as PRIMARY SOURCES for historical research, not as verified historical truths. Any researcher, historian or journalist using these materials must:

  • Cross-check the information with other primary and secondary sources
  • Apply critical methodology
  • Not consider testimonies valid without independent verification
  • Properly cite the source and context

5.4. HISTORICAL AND HUMAN VALUE

Despite the limitations described above, personal testimonies have immense historical and human value. They offer personal perspectives on historical events, emotional and human context, details of everyday life, and voices often excluded from official history.
